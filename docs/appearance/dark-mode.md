# Dark mode

Lumark has a light and a dark theme. Toggle between them with the sun/moon button in the top-right corner of the toolbar, next to the editor mode switcher.

## How the theme is chosen

- **First launch:** Lumark follows your operating system's light or dark setting.
- **After that:** whatever you pick with the toggle is remembered on this device and used every time Lumark starts, even if the OS setting changes.

## What changes

The whole window follows the theme: the folders and notes panels, the editing pane (including its syntax highlighting) and the preview, which switches to GitHub's light or dark Markdown styling.

The theme is only a display setting. It doesn't change your notes.
