# Welcome to Lumark

Lumark is a fast, cross-platform, local-first Markdown note-taking app. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, stores every note as a plain `.md` file on your own disk, and never sends your data anywhere.

![Lumark app demo](/img/lumark-screenshot.gif)

## What you get

- **Local-first.** Notes are ordinary Markdown files in a folder on your computer. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
- **Folders and notes.** Group notes into folders, rename them, delete them, and search within a folder.
- **Split editor.** Write Markdown on the left and see the rendered preview on the right, or focus on just one of them.
- **Autosave.** Every change is saved for you. There is no Save button to forget.
- **Light and dark themes.** Follows your OS on first launch, then remembers your choice.
- **Small and quick.** Built with [Tauri](https://tauri.app), so it starts fast and stays light on memory.
